Portal
The
MaRDI Portal
is the central entry point for MaRDI’s research data infrastructure, which includes services, data, and a highly curated connected mathematical knowledge graph. It is driven by the fact that while there are available many sources research data and related services in mathematics they are hosted as stand-alone solutions and often do not allow for a
FAIR
use. This hinders the development of services for automatic or systematic data retrieval in a standardized way in many areas of the mathematical science.
With the
MaRDI Portal
, we provide metadata of the federated data sources according to the FAIR principles and access to services developed within MaRDI for specific data types. Furthermore, through connections of the different items (data, publications, software, algorithms, models and many more) the
MaRDI Portal
provides access to a very large knowledge graph dedicated to mathematics. Based on the knowledge graph many new services can be built to provide specific solutions for the mathematical community and beyond.
The
MaRDI Portal
includes newly developed and existing data repositories that are integrated into the MaRDI knowledge graph for convenient use and machine access. Through the integration into the MaRDI infrastructure improvements are made at the original data sources. Furthermore important mathematical contributions such as the possibility to search for mathematical formulae at the
MaRDI Portal
are introduced at Wikidata as well because both are based on the same technological platform.
The
MaRDI Portal
allows researchers, mathematicians and those using mathematics, to find and explore information, from multiple sources in the mathematical domain. Modern artificial intelligence (AI) technologies ease the interaction with the available information.
The infrastructure for the front- and backend of the portal is being developed and hosted by
MaRDI’s Task Area TA5
.
The
MaRDI Portal
is the entry point to many of the
services
provided by MaRDI. Whether you are a researcher, a student, or simply someone passionate about mathematics, the MaRDI Portal is here to support your exploration, learning, and discovery with resources tailored to diverse needs and interests. We embrace the open-source philosophy – our source code repositories and detailed technical documentation are freely accessible.

zbMathOpen Articles,
				   October 15, 2026
zbMathOpen is an information service for mathematicians from research and teaching with networked information on mathematical topics, the authors and their publications with references. Besides bibliographic information it provides the user with an overview of the current mathematical literature without having to read all the journals himself. The
MaRDI portal
ensures FAIR access.

Triplets,
				   October 15, 2026
Knowledge graphs store interlinked descriptions of entities, such as objects or concepts, and their relationships, forming triplets that represent semantic connections. Small, domain-specific, and manually curated knowledge graphs typically contain around 100,000 triples, while larger general-purpose graphs, such as Wikidata, contain around 15 billion triples. The
MaRDI knowledge graph
is highly curated and focused on mathematics.
